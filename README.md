### Santiago Beguería

Research Scientist at the Spanish National Research Council ([CSIC](https://www.csic.es)), [Aula Dei Experimental Station (EEAD-CSIC)](https://www.eead.csic.es), Zaragoza, Spain. I co-lead the [Climate and Climate Services Laboratory (LCSC)](https://lcsc.csic.es).

I work on statistical climatology: climate extremes and their physical drivers, Bayesian hierarchical spatio-temporal models for large station networks, drought indices and monitoring, and long, quality-controlled climate datasets. Most of what I develop is released as open code and data.

[ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3974-2947) · [Personal website](https://santiago.begueria.es) · [LCSC](https://lcsc.csic.es) · [LCSC on GitHub](https://github.com/lcsc)

#### Research software

- [**SPEI**](https://github.com/sbegueria/SPEI): R package to compute the Standardized Precipitation-Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI), the Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI) and reference evapotranspiration. On [CRAN](https://cran.r-project.org/package=SPEI).
- [**SPEIbase**](https://github.com/sbegueria/SPEIbase): code used to produce the [SPEI global drought database](https://spei.csic.es/database.html) ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.834462](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.834462)).
- [**homogen**](https://github.com/sbegueria/homogen): change-point detection and homogenisation of daily climate series.
- [**disdRo**](https://github.com/sbegueria/disdRo): tools for disdrometer data (rainfall drop size and kinetic energy).
- [**massmov2d**](https://github.com/sbegueria/massmov2d): numerical model of mass movements over complex topography.
- [**bibliometRics**](https://github.com/sbegueria/bibliometRics): bibliometric analysis of an author or a research team.
- [**SPEI_calc**](https://github.com/sbegueria/SPEI_calc) / [**SPI_calc**](https://github.com/sbegueria/SPI_calc): command-line SPEI and SPI calculators in C.

With colleagues I have also developed [reddPrec](https://cran.r-project.org/package=reddPrec), for the reconstruction of daily precipitation series and grids.

#### Datasets and operational services

- [SPEI Global Drought Monitor](https://spei.csic.es/map) and the [SPEI global database](https://spei.csic.es/database.html).
- [Drought Monitor of Spain](https://monitordesequia.csic.es) and other climate services of the LCSC.
- High-resolution climate datasets for Spain, deposited in [DIGITAL.CSIC](https://digital.csic.es): MOPREDAScentury ([doi:10.5194/essd-15-2547-2023](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-15-2547-2023)), STEAD ([doi:10.5194/essd-11-1171-2019](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-11-1171-2019)), SPREAD ([doi:10.5194/essd-9-721-2017](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-9-721-2017)) and PTI-grid ([doi:10.20350/digitalcsic/22645](https://doi.org/10.20350/digitalcsic/22645)).

Repositories of the laboratory are in [github.com/lcsc](https://github.com/lcsc), including [climind](https://github.com/lcsc/climind) (138 standard climate indices), [FlexDroughtIndex](https://github.com/lcsc/FlexDroughtIndex), [piragua](https://github.com/lcsc/piragua) (PIRAGUA project, water resources of the Pyrenees) and the tools behind our web climate services.
